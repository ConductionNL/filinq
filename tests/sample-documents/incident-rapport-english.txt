1. INCIDENT SUMMARY

On 3 February 2026 at approximately 14:23 CET, a data breach was detected in the customer relationship management (CRM) system of TechFlow Solutions B.V., registered at Keizersgracht 520, 1017 EJ Amsterdam, The Netherlands (Chamber of Commerce: 56789012).

The breach was reported by IT Security Manager David van der Berg (employee ID: TF-2019-088, email: d.vanderberg@techflow.eu, mobile: +31 6 55 44 33 22) to the Data Protection Authority within the mandatory 72-hour window.

3. ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS

The investigation, led by external cybersecurity consultant James O'Brien (CyberShield Consulting Ltd., 45 Baker Street, London W1U 8EW, United Kingdom, j.obrien@cybershield.co.uk), determined that:

a) An SQL injection vulnerability in the customer portal (URL: https://portal.techflow.eu) was exploited.
b) The attacker's IP address was traced to 185.234.xx.xx (anonymized), originating from a VPN service.
c) Database administrator Natasha Volkov (employee ID: TF-2021-156) had flagged the vulnerability in a security audit report dated 15 October 2025 but remediation was delayed.
